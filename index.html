Biography

Orazio Pontorno is a PhD student enrolled in the National PhD in Artificial Intelligence, XXXIX cycle, organised by the Università Campus Bio-Medico di Roma. He holds a bachelor's degree in Mathematics and a master's degree in Data Science from the University of Catania. His career includes research experience in both corporate and academic environments. His research interests include 'Deepfakes Analysis and Detection' and 'AI for Neuroscience'. He enjoys teamwork and is motivated by the goal of continuously improving his knowledge and skills.
